## Requested checkpoint

The request required the existing editable Figma Design file to retain the four
historical Direction A comparison frames unchanged while changing the comparison
selection from Pending to Direction A. The human rationale was:

- the prominent central bloom better communicates the flower, leaf, and water
  identity;
- Direction A feels calmer, more immersive, and more distinctive;
- Direction A's atmosphere should remain primary;
- Direction B's clearer timing language, segmented progress, guidance
  organization, and accessibility cues should be incorporated; and
- no third direction should be created.

The selected expansion had to provide editable static references for entry/setup,
active inhale, active exhale, reduced/No motion, completion with an optional
non-clinical Next-time setup choice, factual history, and independent Support,
with narrow and wide versions wherever layout materially differs.

The design had to retain the central six-petal bloom, blue/green water-and-leaf
identity, six cycles, 4-second inhale, 6-second exhale, visible phase and progress
text, explicit segmented progress, text equivalents, active Stop and Support,
44-pixel-minimum targets, visible-focus intent, contrast, and an understandable
No motion presentation. It had to exclude voice/audio/microphone controls,
clinical claims, monitoring, location inference, authentication, provider work,
hidden Support, source code, and raster interface substitutes.
